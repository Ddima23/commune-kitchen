# Commune Kitchen — AI for small kitchens in Finland & Åland

**Order smart. Check a delivery in seconds. Scale recipes with confidence.**

Built for the kitchens everyone forgot: small communes, co-living houses, B&Bs, campsites and course centres that cook 5–30 covers a day and still run on paper, spreadsheets and gut feel.

## What you get

**5 agent skills**
- Recipe scaling & allergens — scale any recipe to any headcount, convert units, adapt for the 14 EU allergens (with a working calculator).
- Ordering & par sheets — turn a weekly menu into a supplier-ready order sheet.
- Receipt checking — reconcile a delivery against the order in a tick-off checklist.
- Recipe ideas — varied, non-repeating dishes from what's in stock.
- Finnish food compliance — self-monitoring (omavalvonta), temperature logs, allergen labelling, Oiva/ÅMHM inspection readiness.

**3 tools**
- `receipt_parse` — turn a receipt photo's text into structured items (Finnish, Swedish or English).
- `receipt_check` — match the receipt to your order: received / short / missing / wrong / use-first / expired.
- `recipe_suggest` — "what can I cook?" ranked by what's in your fridge.

**A starter recipe library** — 10 dishes (red/white fish, ragùs, dairy dishes, lohikeitto, makaronilaatikko) with allergen data, ready to extend with your own.

## Who it's for
Small communes, co-living kitchens, B&Bs, campsites, course centres and food trucks in Finland, Åland and the Nordics. No coding required.

## FAQ
- **Does it handle Finnish/Swedish receipts?** Yes — S-/K-market, Prisma, Lidl receipts and fi/sv product names are built in (maito→milk, lohifilee→salmon fillet, tomaattimurska→canned tomatoes).
- **Is it food-authority compliant?** It encodes omavalvonta basics and the 14 EU allergens; always confirm against current Ruokavirasto / ÅMHM guidance for your premises.
- **Do I need to code?** No. Install via the Plugins → Discover tab with one index URL.
